# Ideal CRM — Purchasing Domain Notes

## Purchase Order Receiving Report
The Receiving Report prints at the time of PO Receiving and finalizing the Purchase Order.
It lists each part number on the purchase order with quantities received.

## Total On Hand (Tot OH)
Tot OH shows the total quantity on hand across all locations, including the quantity just received.
For serialized products with ordered quantity greater than one, Tot OH may display against each
serial number or only the first serial listed.

## Kits and Non-Inventory items
Kit and Non-Inventory part numbers do not track on-hand quantities, so the Tot OH column must be
blank for those rows.

## Bins and Locations
The Bin/Loc column shows where stock is stored. New columns are typically added before Bin/Loc.
